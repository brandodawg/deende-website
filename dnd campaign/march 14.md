aoi deceives tulgie into opening the door. elise convinces tulgie to explain what happened to her. jeremiah gives tulgie his lantern, and she is unimpressed. tulgie came across many artifacts and stored them in a cave, but beaver steals the dagger that tulgie kept in her house. jeremiah goes in to take a sip of the soup on the table, and elise dunks his head in the scalding broth. jeremiah tames two wolves found under tulgie's bed. however, the wolves love beaver more than jeremiah. elise and aoi bond over shared humanity and shared soup.
### going to croaker cave
josiah gets a map of the cave. elise and jeremiah are playfully mad at each other. elise and aoi talk with each other and jeremiah and beaver talk with each other. the cave is told to be dangerous and abandoned.
### entering croaker cave
beaver, jeremiah, and aoi spot two giant ice frogs in a pool of water below. elise takes aoi across the pool using her grappling hook. aoi flips off jeremiah and beaver as they walk away. beaver tries to sneak his way across the pool but falls off the wall, alerting the frogs below. "they're fighting" aoi tells elise. she can't see shit. beaver kills one of the frogs, and then turns on a light so elise can see. the other frog perishes similarly. "they killed the frogs" aoi says, despite elise being able to see the whole thing. jeremiah and beaver fall into the water. jeremiah gets out of the water, makes it to the other side, and complains to a disinterested aoi. beaver becomes exhausted, and lays in a trance outside the cave for four hours to recover. beaver snores the word "aoooooooi!"
### bandits
