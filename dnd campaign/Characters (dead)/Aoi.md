# Background
## Story
Aoi was born in Nagasaki in 1932 to Hades and a Japanese sailor. Her mother raised her as a sailor, island hopping when World War II began. After her mother died in the Battle of Midway in 1942, Aoi fended for herself on the open seas, eventually returning to Nagasaki in 1944. The day before the bomb dropped, Tralfamadorians take Aoi to Tralfamadore and place her in a geodesic dome as part of a Earth zoo enclosure.

As time is non-linear in the geodesic dome, Aoi continuously relives moments of her past. {how she gets out of the geodesic dome}. {how she connects to the other characters}. When Aoi escaped the geodesic dome, she absorbed psionic powers from the Tralfamadorians, and channels their abilities through her spells.

Aoi is conflicted on whether or not to strive to reintegrate with society. On one hand, she was born as a tiefling, grew up in a war torn society, lost her mother and had to fend for herself as a child, and was taken by the Tralfamadorians. However, if she connects with others, she will escape her past and be given a chance to rewrite the future that the Tralfamadorians planned for her. Aoi must also get over her demoralized state instilled in her by the Tralfamadorians.
