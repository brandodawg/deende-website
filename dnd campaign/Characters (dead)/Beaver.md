# Background
## Story
Centuries ago in the High Forest, the elves fought in the Fifth Crown War. Beaver's father perished when Beaver was an infant. He was the commander of the dark elf army, fighting the wild elves led by Melandrach. Beaver was raised by his mother, who was a prophet to the spider goddess Lolth. After the Crown War ended, the dark elves fled into their underground sanctuary.

In hiding, Lolth began showing Beaver's mother visions of the destruction of the wild elves. Beaver's mother compelled the other dark elves to grow an army in secret to one day overthrow the wild elves. Beaver was sent to spy on Melandrach above ground.

When he reached the surface, he began to realize the wild elves were not brutes, and instead tamed animals. In disguise, Beaver befriended Melandrach, who taught him to communicate with animals. Each night, Beaver would take animals from the surface to a secret safehouse underground. One night, Beaver retreated underground to find his mother possessed by Lolth. She mobilized the dark elf army, and under cover of night the army massacred the entire wild elf civilization above ground. Beaver's mother killed Melandrach to venge her husband's death as Beaver watched.

Horrified, Beaver ran away from his home under the High Forest, taking his animals with him. Soon after he left, his mother discovered his empty animal sanctuary, which was strictly forbidden by Lolth. Beaver knows he would be not be welcome back in the High Forest, so he decided to choose a nomadic lifestyle; he camps underground by day and travels with his animals by night. He is the hero of his animals, yet each night he searches for the final animal he was unable to save from the High Forest: the beaver.
